Every Atrium package comes with room credits. They're how members book the rooms that make the Atrium more than a place to open a laptop: meeting rooms, a production studio and a performance space.

If you're weighing a membership, or you just joined and want to get the most from it, here's how credits work in plain terms.

## What a room credit is

A room credit is worth $10. Members spend credits instead of cash when they book a room, and each room costs a set number of credits per hour.

Think of credits as booking time that's already built into your package. When your team needs a table for an afternoon, you don't reach for a card. You spend credits you already have.

## Which rooms you can book

There are three kinds of bookable rooms at the Atrium:

- **Meeting rooms** for trainings, workshops and team meetings
- **The production studio** for video, photo and podcast production
- **The performance space** for performances, events and large gatherings

Production studio and performance space bookings follow our booking guidelines, so ask about those when you reserve.

## Member and public rates

You don't have to be a member to book. Anyone can use these rooms at public rates. Members book the same rooms with credits.

| Room | Credits an hour | Member cost an hour | Public rate an hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting room | 3 | $30 | $36 |
| Production studio | 5 | $50 | $60 |
| Performance space | 10 | $100 | $125 |

In every room, the member cost is lower than the public rate.

## How many credits each package includes

| Package | Price | Room credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | $150 a month | 6 every month |
| Tier 2 | $400 a month | 18 every month |
| Private office | $850 a month | 108 every quarter |

Tier 1 includes unlimited coworking. Tier 2 adds a dedicated desk. The private office is a lockable office, about 10 by 13 feet, that includes 3 team members.

## When credits refresh

Credits refresh monthly and don't roll over, so it pays to plan your bookings instead of saving them up. Private office packages receive their credits by the quarter: 108 every quarter.

Need more than your package includes? You can add extra credits anytime at $10 each.

## A few examples

Here's what common bookings look like in credits:

- **A 2-hour team meeting** in a meeting room costs 6 credits (3 credits an hour for 2 hours). That's a full month of Tier 1 credits.
- **A 1-hour podcast recording** in the production studio costs 5 credits.
- **A 3-hour gathering** in the performance space costs 30 credits, or $300 in credits. At the public rate, the same 3 hours would be $375.
- **A Tier 2 member** with 18 credits a month could book 6 hours of meeting room time.
- **A private office team** with 108 credits a quarter could book 36 hours of meeting room time, or mix in studio sessions.

## Why we use credits

We built the Atrium so Kingdom-minded businesses, nonprofits and community members could work, meet and create under one roof. The rooms are a big part of that. They're where a team trains, a message gets recorded and people gather around something that matters.

Credits keep it simple. Your package covers your regular room needs, the math is easy, and the rooms stay open to the wider community at public rates.
