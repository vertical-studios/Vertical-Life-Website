If you run a business or nonprofit with faith at its center, you've probably felt the split. One version of you on Sunday, another on Monday.

The Atrium exists so those two can finally meet. It's the physical home of Vertical Life, on N Meridian near Carmel at 10330 N Meridian St, Suite 300, Indianapolis, IN 46290. Inside, you'll find coworking, dedicated desks and private offices, all in one building, on purpose.

## Who the Atrium is for

The Atrium is built for Kingdom-minded businesses, nonprofits, educational initiatives and community members who want to work alongside people who share their compass.

If you work on your own, the solo memberships fit. If you lead a team, a private office gives you a room with a door that locks. Either way, you're part of the same community.

The Atrium is also home base for Vertical Life, the parent brand of Vertical Immersive, Vertical Wealth and Vertical Studios.

## The three packages

Every package includes the community. Here's what each one adds:

| Package | Price | What you get | Room credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | $150 a month | Unlimited coworking | 6 every month |
| Tier 2 | $400 a month | A dedicated desk plus unlimited coworking | 18 every month |
| Private office | $850 a month | A lockable office, about 10 by 13 feet, with 3 team members included | 108 every quarter |

Private office teams can add more people for $100 a month each. There are 15 private offices available.

Room credits are worth $10 each. Members use them to book meeting rooms, the production studio and the performance space. Anyone else can book those rooms at public rates.

## How to choose

Start by counting people, then count meetings.

- **Choose Tier 1** if you're one person who needs a place to work without a fixed seat. Your 6 credits cover a 2-hour team meeting each month.
- **Choose Tier 2** if you want a desk that's yours every day. Your 18 credits cover about 6 hours of meeting room time a month.
- **Choose a private office** if you have a team or need a space you can lock. Three team members are included, and 108 credits a quarter gives your team room to meet, record or gather.

Not sure yet? That's what a tour is for.

## What makes faith-integrated coworking different

Most coworking spaces sell desks. The Atrium is built around a conviction: people flourish when faith, purpose and work are integrated rather than separated, and people grow best in community, not isolation.

You've probably sat in the meeting where you softened a conviction to keep things comfortable. You may have prayed about a decision alone, then made it alone. That's normal. It isn't necessary.

Here's what that looks like day to day:

- **A community that shares your compass.** Faith-led builders who show up for each other. Not a networking group. Not a small group. A working ecosystem.
- **Seminars and workshops.** Learning and collaboration with people who share your values. Your next collaborator might work down the hall.
- **Rooms most offices don't have.** Meeting rooms, a production studio and a performance space, on site.

Our values are Intention, Service, Innovation, Transformation and Achievement. Our tagline, Achieving Immeasurably More, comes from Ephesians 3:20: God is able to do far more than we ask or imagine. That's the posture we bring to Monday morning.
